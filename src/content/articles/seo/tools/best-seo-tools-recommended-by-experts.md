If you’re trying to figure out the **best SEO tool** for your business, this blog should save you time and money.

**None of the links in this article are affiliate links.**

**This is not a sales pitch.**

It’s a collection of honest answers from 40 [SEO gurus](/quick-reads/best-seo-experts/) I respect & trust.

I asked them a simple question:

**“Give me one SEO tool you actually use, but it can’t be Ahrefs, Semrush, Google Search Console, or a tool you own.”**

The answers came from a mix of agency owners, consultants, in-house SEOs, and technical specialists.

Many of them have attended at my conference *[The Masterminders](https://themasterminders.com/)*, or are part of private SEO WhatsApp groups I’m involved in.

The idea was to cut through the obvious answers and get real recommendations that might otherwise get overlooked.

Some tools are well-known, others are underrated gems, but every response came from genuine day-to-day use.

Let’s face it, SEOs love a new tool.

If something promises better audits or smarter keyword insights, we’re all tempted to try it.

But constantly testing new platforms can get expensive fast.

And asking this question might have just hurt my bank account more than I expected.

**Important**: These tools worked for the people I asked, but that doesn’t mean they’re right for you.

**Always do your own research before buying anything.**

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best SEO tool in 2025?

There’s no single “best” SEO tool for everyone.

It depends on what you need.

For keyword research, many SEOs like Keyword Insights or Data For SEO.

For [technical audits](/seo/technical-seo/audit/), tools like Screaming Frog, Sitebulb, and JetOctopus stood out in this list.

The best tool is the one that fits your workflow and goals.

### Can I do SEO with free tools only?

Yes, but with limitations.

Tools like Google Search Console and Ubersuggest offer free features, but most SEOs eventually invest in paid tools for deeper insights, automation, and time-saving.

If budget is tight, start free and upgrade as you grow.

### What tools are best for content optimisation?

Tools like Surfer SEO, NeuronWriter, and Writesonic were popular picks.

They help align your content with what ranks by analysing top-performing pages, NLP terms, and SERP intent.

These tools are useful for both agencies and solo creators.

### Are all-in-one SEO platforms better than specialised tools?

All-in-one tools like SE Ranking or Semrush are convenient, but many SEOs prefer using a mix of specialised tools to go deeper in key areas like outreach, technical audits, or on-page SEO.

It often comes down to your experience level and budget.

### What should I consider before choosing an SEO tool?

Focus on your actual needs.

Are you doing technical audits?

Outreach? Content writing?

Also consider support, pricing, and whether the tool integrates with your stack.

Asking other SEOs (like in this roundup) is a great place to start.
