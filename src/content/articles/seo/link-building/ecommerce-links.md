**E-commerce link building → earns → backlinks that increase product visibility, authority, and conversions.**  
Because e-commerce sites rely heavily on product and category pages, gaining quality backlinks helps boost rankings where it matters most, at the transactional level.

In this guide by **Kasra Dash**, you’ll learn the most effective ways to build backlinks for your e-commerce store, avoid common pitfalls, and measure the ROI of your campaigns.

**Ecommerce link building is about more than rankings, it’s about creating pathways that lead customers directly to purchase.**

## What Is E-commerce Link Building?

**E-commerce link building → is → the process of acquiring backlinks to an online store’s category, product, or blog pages to improve search visibility and authority.**  
Unlike traditional websites, e-commerce platforms require links that balance commercial and informational intent.

### Why It Matters

- **Boosts Product Rankings:** Backlinks signal product authority and relevance.
- **Improves Domain Trust:** Strengthens your overall brand reputation.
- **Drives Referral Traffic:** Attracts ready-to-buy customers from other trusted sources.

**Relevant backlinks → generate → both SEO and direct revenue impact.**

**Strong link profiles amplify every other SEO effort across your e-commerce ecosystem.**

### Are e-commerce backlinks different from regular backlinks?

Yes, e-commerce backlinks are different because they target transactional pages, requiring higher relevance and conversion-focused placement to avoid appearing manipulative.

## Why E-commerce Sites Struggle with Link Building

Building backlinks to e-commerce websites is harder than for content-led sites, but understanding why helps you overcome those challenges.

### 1. Product Pages Lack Linkability

Most people prefer linking to informational content rather than commercial pages. Product pages must therefore offer additional value, such as unique product data, guides, or comparisons.

### 2. Seasonal and Expired Products

Out-of-stock products create broken link risks. Use redirects or evergreen category URLs to preserve link equity.

### 3. Competitive Niches

Popular sectors like fashion, electronics, or supplements are oversaturated, requiring more creative outreach and content strategies.

**Linkability gap → limits → traditional outreach success.**

→ Learn alternative methods in [**Link Building Strategies**](/seo/link-building/).

**Solving linkability challenges turns e-commerce from a hard sell into a link magnet.**

### Why do few people link to product pages?

Few people link to product pages because they see them as purely commercial, but adding educational value or original data increases link-worthiness.

## Best E-commerce Link Building Strategies

There’s no one-size-fits-all approach. The best ecommerce backlink strategies combine creativity, relevance, and ethical outreach.

### 1. Create Linkable Assets

Use your e-commerce blog or resource section to publish high-value, linkable content:

- **Buying guides** (“Best Running Shoes for 2025”)
- **Comparison posts** (“Nike vs Adidas Trail Shoes”)
- **Data-led studies** (“Top 10 Sustainable Fashion Brands”)

**Content-led resources → attract → editorial backlinks naturally.**

→ For content planning, see **Content Frameworks**.

**Educational content drives backlinks, boosting both SEO and customer trust.**

### Should I build links to product or content pages?

You should build links to both, as informational pages attract backlinks while product and category pages drive conversions.

### 2. Leverage Digital PR Campaigns

Collaborate with journalists and niche publications to secure coverage. Share data insights, customer trends, or social impact stories related to your brand.

Example campaign ideas:

- “Most Eco-Friendly Packaging in UK Retail 2025”
- “How AI Personalisation Is Changing Online Shopping”

Tools like **HARO**, **Qwoted**, and **PressPlugs** can connect you with media opportunities.

**Digital PR → earns → high-authority backlinks and brand mentions.**

**Strong stories earn both media exposure and SEO equity.**

### Is digital PR expensive?

Yes, digital PR can be resource-intensive, but it’s cost-effective long-term because editorial links from trusted media domains deliver lasting authority.

### 3. Product Roundups and Gift Guides

Pitch your products for inclusion in “Best of” or “Holiday Gift” articles. Editors frequently update these lists and welcome new product suggestions.

Example search operators:

```
"best [product] for [audience]"
"holiday gift guide" + [category]
"top products" + [keyword]
```

→ Support this tactic with [**Guest Posting**](/seo/link-building/guest-posting/) for brand storytelling opportunities.

**Roundup features → drive → backlinks and direct sales simultaneously.**

**Relevance and timing make roundups powerful link-building tools.**

### How do I get featured in gift guides?

You can get featured by contacting editors early in seasonal cycles, offering product samples, and providing professional product imagery.

### 4. Collaborate with Influencers

Influencers bridge the gap between SEO and social proof.  
Partner with micro-influencers whose content aligns with your brand. Offer affiliate codes, discounts, or collaborations that encourage organic mentions.

**Influencer collaboration → generates → backlinks, traffic, and brand awareness.**

**Authenticity drives trust, which drives links.**

### Do influencer backlinks count for SEO?

Yes, influencer backlinks count when placed on public websites or blogs because they transfer link equity, unlike links from social platforms.

### 5. Recover Lost and Broken Links

Monitor for 404 errors on discontinued or relocated products. Redirect expired URLs to updated category pages or equivalents.

Tools to use:

- **Ahrefs Broken Links Report**
- **Semrush Backlink Audit**
- **Google Search Console**

→ Step-by-step guide: [**Broken Link Building**](/seo/link-building/broken-link-building/)

**Fixing broken links → restores → lost equity and improves site health.**

**Link recovery is as important as link acquisition.**

### Should I redirect old product pages?

Yes, always redirect discontinued products to the most relevant page to preserve authority and prevent user frustration.

### 6. Build Partnerships with Suppliers and Retailers

Suppliers, partners, and distributors often feature brand directories. Request inclusion and ensure your listing includes a dofollow link.

**B2B collaboration → expands → referral networks and link reach.**

**Partnerships compound trust across your supply chain.**

### How do I ask for a supplier link?

Ask politely by highlighting mutual benefit: your listing improves visibility for both brands and strengthens ongoing collaboration.

### 7. Run Affiliate and Loyalty Programmes

Reward bloggers, partners, and customers for sharing your products. Use **nofollow** attributes for paid affiliate links to comply with Google’s policies.

**Affiliate systems → increase → organic mentions and brand loyalty.**

**Ethical incentives attract genuine advocacy, not manipulation.**

### Are affiliate backlinks good for SEO?

Affiliate backlinks don’t directly pass SEO value because of the nofollow tag, but they build brand mentions that indirectly boost authority.

Audit your e-commerce backlinks monthly because e-commerce sites experience faster link turnover due to seasonal campaigns and product changes.

## Avoiding Common E-commerce Link Building Mistakes

Link building success depends on integrity and precision. Avoid shortcuts that can harm your rankings or reputation.

### Common Mistakes

1. Buying backlinks or using PBNs.
2. Building links to discontinued or 404 pages.
3. [Overusing exact-match anchors](/seo/link-building/anchor-text-optimisation/) on product pages.
4. Ignoring site speed and technical SEO foundations.
5. Neglecting follow-up and relationship nurturing.

→ Use the **Technical SEO Audit** checklist to ensure technical readiness.

**Spammy backlinks → trigger → algorithmic distrust and ranking drops.**

**Ethical link building safeguards your e-commerce brand for the long term.**

### Can Google penalise e-commerce sites for link building?

Yes, Google can penalise e-commerce sites that use manipulative link schemes because unnatural link patterns violate its Webmaster Guidelines.
