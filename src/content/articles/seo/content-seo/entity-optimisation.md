## What Is Entity Optimisation?

**Entity optimisation → focuses → on improving the clarity and accuracy of concepts within your content.**

Entities are not just keywords, they are people, places, organisations, or topics that search engines can distinctly identify.

**Defined entities → strengthen → machine understanding of relevance.**

For example:

- *“Kasra Dash”* (entity: person) → *“is an SEO consultant”* (relationship: occupation) → *“based in the UK”* (attribute: location).

→ Deepen your understanding with [**Topical Authority**](/seo/content-seo/topical-authority/).

Keywords tell Google what; entities tell it who, where, and why.

### What is entity optimisation?

Entity optimisation is the process of structuring and clarifying the people, places, brands, and topics in your content so search engines can accurately understand and rank them.

## How to Optimise Content for Entities (Step-by-Step)

### Step 1: Identify Core Entities

List all relevant people, tools, and topics tied to your content’s theme. Use **Google’s Knowledge Graph Search API**, **InLinks**, or **WordLift** to extract entities.

**Entity identification → defines → conceptual scope.**

### Step 2: Map Relationships

Connect entities logically through internal links, schema, and contextual references.

**Relationship mapping → creates → semantic cohesion.**

→ Apply this process through [**Internal Linking**](/seo/content-seo/internal-linking/).

### Step 3: Implement Schema Markup

Add structured data (e.g., *Person*, *Organisation*, *Article*) to reinforce entity recognition.

**Schema markup → validates → entity relevance for Google’s crawlers.**

→ Use implementation tips from the [**Schema Markup Guide**](seo/technical-seo/schema-markup-guide/).

### Step 4: Use Consistent Naming and References

Maintain consistent spelling, context, and descriptions across all pages.

**Consistency → confirms → entity authenticity.**

### Step 5: Link to Authoritative Sources

Cite credible external entities to improve your content’s trustworthiness.

**External linking → contextualises → your entity within a trusted network.**

→ Learn auditing process in [**Content Audits**](/seo/content-seo/content-audits/).
