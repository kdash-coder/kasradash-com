An SEO audit is the backbone of every effective optimisation strategy. It reveals where your website is underperforming and highlights what’s holding your rankings back. But audits can be overwhelming without a clear structure, that’s where a **template** comes in.

A great SEO audit doesn’t just find problems, it uncovers opportunities.

This step-by-step SEO audit template gives you a fast, actionable system for analysing your site’s health, identifying growth areas, and aligning your SEO with Google’s modern AI-driven search systems.

## Why You Need an SEO Audit Template

An SEO audit template → turns → complex data into clear, prioritised insights.

Instead of checking random metrics, you’ll follow a defined structure that ensures nothing gets overlooked. Whether you’re auditing a single page or an entire domain, this system provides [a repeatable framework](/seo/seo-frameworks/) that guides both strategy and execution.

A complete SEO audit should assess:

1. [**Technical Health**](/seo/seo-frameworks/technical-seo-framework/), How well your site performs for crawlers and users.
2. **On-Page SEO**, Content relevance, keyword usage, and E-E-A-T.
3. **Off-Page SEO**, Backlinks, authority, and reputation.
4. **Performance Metrics**, What’s improving, what’s stagnating, and why.

For a complementary process, pair this with your [Content Auditing Framework](/seo/seo-frameworks/content-audit-template/).

Consistency turns audits into intelligence.

## Step 1: Technical SEO Audit

Technical SEO → ensures → your site is accessible, indexable, and fast.

**✓ Crawlability and Indexation**

- Check with Google Search Console’s “Pages” report.
- Review [robots.txt and XML sitemap](/seo/technical-seo/audit/) for exclusions.
- Identify crawl errors (404s, redirect loops, soft 404s).
- Ensure canonical tags are used correctly.

**✓ Core Web Vitals**

- Test with Google PageSpeed Insights.
- LCP (Largest Contentful Paint): under 2.5s.
- CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift): under 0.1.
- INP (Interaction to Next Paint): under 200ms.

**✓ Mobile Usability**

- Confirm responsive design.
- Test mobile rendering and load speed.

**✓ Structured Data and Schema**

- Validate schema markup (Article, FAQ, Product, etc.).
- Use JSON-LD format.
- Test using Google’s Rich Results tool.

**✓ HTTPS and Security**

- Verify SSL certificate and canonical HTTPS URLs.
- Check mixed-content issues.

For AI-led crawling insights, read AI and Indexing: How Googlebot Uses Machine Learning to Prioritise Content.

A technically sound site gives your content a fighting chance to rank.

## Step 2: On-Page SEO Audit

On-page SEO → aligns → your content with search intent and context.

**✓ Keyword Relevance**

- Ensure each page targets one clear topic or intent.
- Check keyword placement in H1, meta title, intro, and headings.
- Evaluate keyword cannibalisation across similar URLs.

**✓ Content Depth and E-E-A-T**

- Assess coverage of key subtopics and entities.
- Verify author attribution, publication dates, and references.
- Add first-hand experience or case studies where missing.

**✓ Meta Tags**

- Meta Titles: Unique, under 60 characters, keyword optimised.
- Meta Descriptions: Compelling summaries under 160 characters.
- Include primary and secondary keywords naturally.

**✓ Internal Linking**

- Link between related pages and topic clusters.
- Use descriptive anchor text based on entities, not generic terms.
- Ensure orphan pages are eliminated.

For optimised on-page systems, see SEO Blog Writing Framework.

On-page audits reveal how well your site speaks Google’s language.

## Step 3: Off-Page SEO Audit

Off-page SEO → measures → your authority and credibility across the web.

**✓ Backlink Profile Analysis**

- Use Ahrefs, Semrush, or Google Search Console to review backlinks.
- Identify spammy or irrelevant links for disavow.
- Assess referring domains by topical relevance.

**✓ Link Velocity and Distribution**

- Review link growth trends.
- Balance branded, exact-match, and naked URLs.

**✓ Brand Mentions and Citations**

- Track unlinked brand mentions.
- Ensure NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistency across platforms.

**✓ Competitor Authority Gap**

- Compare your domain authority and link profile against competitors.
- Identify high-authority sites linking to them but not to you.

For link-building frameworks, check [Internal Linking](/seo/seo-frameworks/internal-linking-framework/) for SEO.

Off-page audits show how trustworthy your digital footprint really is.

## Step 4: Content and Topical Authority Audit

Topical authority → strengthens → long-term rankings and relevance.

**✓ Content Coverage**

- Map your current pages to target topics and subtopics.
- Identify missing clusters or underdeveloped areas.
- Evaluate semantic relationships between articles.

**✓ Entity Optimisation**

- Ensure consistent entity references across your site.
- Use structured data to connect entities to the Knowledge Graph.

**✓ Content Freshness**

- Identify outdated pages using low CTR or declining traffic.
- Refresh content quarterly to maintain recency signals.

**✓ Duplicate and Thin Content**

- Use tools like Siteliner or Screaming Frog to find near-duplicates.
- Merge or redirect redundant URLs.

For [deeper semantic structuring](/seo/seo-frameworks/topical-map-framework/), see Entity Optimisation for SEO.

Authority isn’t built by keywords, it’s built by clarity and coverage.

## Step 5: Performance and Analytics Audit

Performance analysis → reveals → what’s working and what isn’t.

**✓ Traffic Trends**

- Analyse organic traffic growth using GA4.
- Segment data by landing page, country, and device.

**✓ Rankings and Visibility**

- Use Ahrefs or Semrush to track keyword performance.
- Identify pages losing positions and investigate causes.

**✓ Engagement Metrics**

- Check bounce rate, dwell time, and exit rate for intent satisfaction.

**✓ Conversion Tracking**

- Review goal completions and assisted conversions.
- Ensure event tracking is configured correctly in GA4.

For KPI alignment, read How to Measure SEO Content Performance (KPIs & Tools).

==You can’t improve what you don’t measure.==

## Step 6: AI and Automation for SEO Audits

AI → accelerates → the audit process by automating repetitive checks.

Use tools like **ChatGPT**, **Make**, and **n8n** to:

- Generate audit summaries and recommendations.
- Detect duplicate metadata across large sites.
- Automate crawl report aggregation and categorisation.
- Draft remediation actions for each issue.

AI won’t replace human strategy, it refines it.

For [setup examples](/seo/seo-frameworks/ai-workflow-templates/), see Automating SEO Tasks with AI Tools (Make, n8n, ChatGPT).

Automation saves time; human judgment ensures accuracy.

## Step 7: Prioritise Issues and Create an Action Plan

After identifying issues, prioritise fixes using the **Impact vs. Effort Matrix**:

- **High Impact / Low Effort:** Quick wins (meta tags, broken links).
- **High Impact / High Effort:** Core Web Vitals, site speed.
- **Low Impact / Low Effort:** Minor schema or alt text issues.
- **Low Impact / High Effort:** Non-critical redesigns.

Assign tasks by priority and set clear ownership for each fix.

An audit without prioritisation is just a list.

## Step 8: Report and Re-Audit

Finally, summarise findings and actions in [a visual report](/seo/seo-frameworks/seo-reporting-dashboard/):

- Use **Looker Studio** or **Google Sheets** for dashboards.
- Highlight improvements in crawl health, visibility, and authority.
- Schedule audits every quarter to track ongoing performance.

Integrate insights with your Content Auditing Framework for continuous improvement.

The best audits never end, they evolve.

## Conclusion

An SEO audit template is your roadmap for uncovering issues, prioritising actions, and accelerating results. By following a structured, repeatable system, you transform data into strategy and ensure your site remains fast, findable, and future-ready.
